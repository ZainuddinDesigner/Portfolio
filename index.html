Introduction

About Zainuddin, interior designer

I design interiors that look beautiful on paper and hold up on site — where creative vision meets an engineer's focus on detail, cost and time.

Hi, I'm Zainuddin, an interior designer who brings a practical edge to the creative process. With a background as a site engineer, I don't just design beautiful spaces — I make sure they're executed seamlessly.

That dual perspective lets me anticipate and solve construction challenges early, guaranteeing high-quality finishes and projects delivered on time and within budget. Away from the drawing board, you'll find me behind a camera or at the movies.

Aarna Villa

Residence

Residential2025

Design meets quiet luxury in this architectural project, where refined materiality pairs with bold, sculptural forms. Warm neutrals, dark woods and polished marble connect the exterior facade seamlessly to the interiors. Structural louvres and geometric screens contrast soft textiles; recessed LED coves and statement crystal lighting sculpt shadow, depth and warmth. Integrated joinery and dark-glass wardrobes optimise space, while brushed brass and textured stone make it a refined, timeless sanctuary.

Brown Teak

The classic café

Commercial2025

Rooted in desert landscapes, this project merges raw earthiness with refined architectural minimalism through warm rammed earth, terracotta plaster and fluted oak. Curved geometry and organic stone lighting soften the bold structure, while expansive glass frames sweeping views of the surrounding dunes. Finished with tactile bouclé, travertine and terrazzo, it harmonises natural elements with contemporary hospitality — soft ambient light accentuates raw wall textures as day turns to night, and native greenery brings life into a grounded, tranquil interior.

04. Warm modernist home

Meadow Croft

Residential design

Residential2026

Designed around industrial sophistication and edgy modern art, this interior fuses sleek architectural lines with bold, expressive character. Deep charcoal tones, exposed plaster textures and rich natural wood floors form a moody, luxurious backdrop. Large-scale street-art murals and classical bust artwork inject an urban edge into the open living spaces, while linear track lighting, sleek dark cabinetry and custom glass enclosures sharpen it. Warm terracotta accents, plush modular seating and botanical displays soften the structure — a refined residential sanctuary bridging high-end minimalism.

Moddy's

The chocolate café — Kodaikanal

Commercial2026

A complete new retail outlet for Moddy's Chocolates in Kodaikanal, expanding the iconic Ooty-based brand into a new hill-station market. The interior pairs hill-station character — scalloped gables, arched display niches and sage-green millwork — with warm oak, terrazzo and rattan, bringing a café, chocolate boutique and patisserie together under one timber-trussed roof.

How I work

From brief
to built.

A design process shaped by site experience — so what's drawn is what gets built, on time and within budget.

01

Brief & Site

Understanding how you live or trade, then surveying the site early to catch structural and service constraints before they become costly.

  • Client brief
  • Site survey
  • Budget
02

Space Planning

Zoning, circulation and furniture layouts drafted in AutoCAD and Revit — every programme area numbered and resolved.

  • AutoCAD
  • Revit BIM
  • Zoning
03

Mood & Material

Mood boards that lock the palette — stone, timber, fabric and light — with finishes chosen for beauty, durability and cost.

  • Mood boards
  • Finishes
  • Lighting
04

3D Visualisation

Photoreal renders and axonometric cutaways in SketchUp, 3ds Max, V-Ray and Enscape so every decision is seen before it's built.

  • SketchUp
  • 3ds Max
  • Enscape
05

Site Execution

As a site engineer, I coordinate contractors and details on the ground — guaranteeing high-quality finishes delivered on time.

  • Coordination
  • Quality control
  • Handover
